Thanks for testing Skärgård Express

This is an early build of an island courier game: you carry parcels round a small Swedish archipelago on a three-wheeled flakmoped. It's playable from start to finish, but it's rough in places, and what you notice is exactly what's needed.

Installing and starting it

  1. Download the zip (about 6 GB). Google Drive may say it can't scan a file this big for viruses; choose Download anyway.
  2. Unzip the whole thing somewhere with about 6 GB free, such as your Documents or a games folder (not inside Program Files). You'll get a folder called SkargardExpress.
  3. Open it and run SkargardExpress.exe.
  4. The game isn't signed yet, so Windows may show Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. Some antivirus programs are wary of new games too.
  5. If it won't start at all, run Engine\Extras\Redist\en-us\vc_redist.x64.exe from the same folder once (it installs the Visual C++ runtime) and try again.
The first start takes a while, and the first minute or so of play may stutter while the graphics card prepares its shaders. It settles down, and it's quicker the next time.

The game starts full screen. Alt+Enter, F11 or Screen in the menu put it in a window. Your career saves as you go; New career on the title screen starts over.

You'll need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • A DirectX 12 graphics card: about an NVIDIA RTX 2060 or AMD RX 5700, or better, with 6 GB of video memory.
  • 16 GB of memory.
  • A keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad (Xbox layout).

What to try

Play however you like; this is just a list of things it would help to hear about. Tick off what you get to.

  • The start. Play the short tutorial on the home quay, from the title screen to your first delivery. Was it clear what to do?
  • Riding. Get a feel for the moped on asphalt, gravel, grass and mud. Try the three cameras (C). Does it handle the way you'd expect? Is it fun?
  • A full day of jobs. Take jobs from the dispatch board (Tab), load up, deliver, and keep going until evening. Do the pay and the parcels' condition make sense?
  • Looking after the load. Brake hard, take corners fast, and ride in the rain. Do parcels slide and spoil in a way that feels fair?
  • The garage. Buy an upgrade in the garage at the harbour office (G there, or from the job list). Is the difference noticeable?
  • Ferries. Ride the ferry to Hamno, riding the moped and on foot. Try the Cabin Pass and the charter barge once you can afford them.
  • On foot. Get off (F), carry parcels by hand (E), visit the village shop.
  • Side quests. Try a few from the list, such as Sixten the lost dog, the wedding cake, the runaway sheep, the moose in the road, the new flagpole or the island-hopping postie.
  • Weather and time. Ride at dusk and at night, in rain and in snow. Can you see where you're going?
  • The islanders. Watch them loading and taking in parcels, and listen to their voices. Does anyone sound wrong for who they are?
  • The music. It's composed in the game. Tell us what you think.
  • Accidents. Tip over, drive off a pier, get stuck. Can you always carry on?

Reporting a problem

Use the feedback link (there's a form for bugs and one for general impressions), or send a message to whoever invited you. For a bug, it helps to know:

  • What you were doing, where (which island, near what), and what went wrong.
  • Whether it happens every time, and how to make it happen again.
  • A screenshot (Win+Shift+S) or a short clip (Win+G opens the Xbox Game Bar recorder).
  • Your graphics card, memory and Windows version (Start, type dxdiag, and look at the System and Display tabs).

And please attach the game's log, which records what the game was doing:

  • SkargardExpress\SkargardExpress\Saved\Logs\SkargardExpress.log inside the folder you unzipped, or
  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkargardExpress\Saved\Logs\SkargardExpress.log (paste that into the Explorer address bar).

The log is replaced each time the game starts, so grab it straight after the problem (older ones are kept beside it with a date in the name). If the game crashes, send the newest folder under Saved\Crashes too. The log holds your PC's hardware details and Windows user folder name, but nothing else personal.

How smooth is it? Press the ` key (above Tab) to open the console, type stat fps and press Enter to show the frame rate. Type it again to hide it.

Known issues

Already on the list, so no need to report these (but do say if they spoil the game for you):

  • The moose is sculpted rather than a proper model, and the sheep's fleece looks a bit like plaster up close.
  • Clothes can clip at the waist when people walk.
  • The courier walks over low parcels on the ground rather than nudging them along.
  • Granite on the coast looks flat from high up.
  • It's a development build, so the odd technical message may flash up on screen.

What's in this build

:

  • Three islands and four skerries, with a scheduled road ferry, footbridges and a cable ferry, a Cabin Pass and a charter barge.
  • A campaign in four chapters, daily jobs from the dispatch board, island reputation, and garage upgrades.
  • 36 side quests, with special loads (a flagpole, gas bottles, a cat in a carrier, a pizza stack, a buoy), herding sheep and a moose in the road.
  • Loose physical cargo that slides, gets knocked, soaks in the rain and spoils; parcels that come out of the sea with you are a little worse for it.
  • Clear weather, rain, snow, storms, autumn leaves, and day and night, through the seasons.
  • Islanders who load and take in parcels by hand, visitors out walking and cycling, natural voices, and music composed in the game.
